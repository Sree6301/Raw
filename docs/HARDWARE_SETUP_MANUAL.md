# HP Smart Tank 589 Hardware Setup Manual

## 1. Physical Connectivity

### USB Setup (Recommended)
1. Connect the high-speed USB-B cable to the rear port of the HP Smart Tank 589.
2. Plug the USB-A end into a native USB 3.0/2.0 port on the Windows 11 PC.
3. Open Windows Settings $\rightarrow$ Bluetooth & Devices $\rightarrow$ Printers & Scanners.
4. Verify that `HP Smart Tank 580-590 series` appears with status **Ready** on port `USB001`.

### Wi-Fi / Wireless Setup
1. Press and hold the Wireless button on the printer control panel for 3 seconds until the Wi-Fi icon blinks.
2. Connect the printer to the shop Wi-Fi network using WPS or HP Smart utility.
3. Verify that `HP Smart Tank 580-590 series [5C4AE8]` appears on port `WSD-...`.

## 2. Paper Media Handling

The HP Smart Tank 589 uses a rear gravity feed paper tray.

### Profile 1: Normal A4 Paper (Document Xerox & Duplex)
- Paper specifications: 75 GSM - 80 GSM Plain Copy Paper.
- Sheet dimensions: $210 \times 297$ mm.
- Tray capacity: Up to 100 sheets.
- Adjust paper-width guide firmly against the left edge of the paper stack.

### Profile 2: Glossy Photo Paper A4
- Paper specifications: 180 GSM - 250 GSM High-Gloss Inkjet Photo Paper.
- Sheet dimensions: $210 \times 297$ mm.
- Tray capacity: Up to 25 sheets.
- Load with glossy side facing **forward/upwards**.

### Profile 3: 4 × 6 inch Photo Paper
- Paper specifications: $101.6 \times 152.4$ mm ($4 \times 6$ in) Glossy Photo Paper.
- Slide paper-width guide inwards to the 4×6 index notch.
- Load with glossy print side facing **forward**.

## 3. Flatbed Scanner Operation

1. Lift the flatbed scanner lid.
2. Ensure the glass platen is clean and free of smudges, fingerprints, or dust.
3. Align physical document face-down with the top-right alignment arrow on the glass bezel.
4. Close scanner lid gently.
5. In the Admin Dashboard or Windows Agent, click **"Scan Page on Glass"**.
6. When complete, flip to the next physical sheet if processing a multi-page scan order.
